About me

I’m a product designer based in Curitiba, Brazil. I make complex B2B and AI products simple to use.

I started in 2012 at a local agency, building websites. In 2013 I moved to Zênite to work on web design and marketing, and from 2016 to 2017 I ran my own web design business. Then I joined Gatefy through marketing and ended up as the only designer of an email security platform, learning product design by doing. Since then I’ve designed a monitoring platform for UNICEF’s Seal program and, at Claro, two products that I later merged into a single AI platform.

I usually work as the only designer on the team, from discovery to delivery, side by side with POs and developers. I like building design systems from scratch, and I’ve done it three times. At Claro I worked on up to six products at once and also took on Product Owner responsibilities.

Away from the screen, I’m usually on a motorcycle or planning the next trip.
